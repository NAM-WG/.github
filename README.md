# New Approach Methodologies (NAMs) & Quantitative Pharmacology Working Group

### ISoP Quantitative Systems Pharmacology (QSP) Special Interest Group

Welcome to the GitHub organization for the **NAMs & Quantitative Pharmacology Working Group**, established under the International Society of Pharmacometrics (ISoP) QSP SIG.

## Mission

Our mission is to advance the integration of **New Approach Methodologies (NAMs)** with quantitative pharmacology to support human-relevant, model-informed decision-making in drug discovery and development.

## Key Objectives

- Explore applications of QSP/QST, PBPK, PK/PD, and other mechanistic modeling approaches to integrate and translate data from human-relevant in vitro NAM platforms.
- Advance quantitative modeling approaches as **in silico NAMs**.
- Develop case studies, shared frameworks, and best practices for NAM applications.
- Foster multidisciplinary collaboration among quantitative pharmacologists, experimental NAM scientists, and other scientific communities.
- Explore integration of AI/ML with mechanistic modeling and NAM data.

## GitHub Organization

This organization serves as a collaborative space for sharing resources, meeting materials, discussions, and project-specific repositories supporting the working group's activities.
